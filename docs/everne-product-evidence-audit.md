## Required evidence intake — do not populate from guesses

1. Authenticated dated quote for exact SKU/variant and quantities, plus evidence of actual current stock.
2. Sample inspection notes including material, label photographs, fit, wash/care and defects.
3. Written commercial photography/description usage rights or independently produced true-to-product photos.
4. Dated courier label rate for Poland → German customer plus packaging/bundling/tracking terms.
5. Actual return destination, customer return charge, supplier defect handling and merchant-funded refund process.
6. Legal product-safety/textile documentation and the final seller's customer-support channel.
7. Per-unit contribution under realistic returns and taxes; no launch on a gross-margin calculation alone.
8. Shopify test order, refund and tracking using the chosen integration; founder's separate written GO decision.
