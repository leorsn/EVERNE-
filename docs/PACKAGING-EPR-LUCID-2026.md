# EVERNE — Packaging / EPR / LUCID Gate (Germany, 2026)

Status: **PRE-LAUNCH / HARD BLOCKER BEFORE FIRST SHIPMENT**

Date: 29 September 2026

This checklist reflects the packaging-law framework in force after the 12 August 2026 transition to the PPWR / German VerpackDG structure. Do not rely on an old static VerpackG checklist.

## 1. Determine EVERNE's role — BLOCKER

For every packaging layer used in the German market, determine whether EVERNE is the responsible manufacturer / producer under the current rules.

Relevant examples:

- product packaging bearing EVERNE branding
- sleeves / boxes / pouches used for the Care Set
- shipping cartons
- shipping bags
- paper/cardboard inserts
- tissue paper
- plastic film
- void fill
- tape and other dispatch packaging

If EVERNE or its fulfilment partner fills shipment packaging for delivery to end consumers, that dispatch packaging requires a dedicated legal assessment. For typical online shipping packaging, system participation is generally required.

## 2. LUCID registration — BLOCKER

Before packaging for which EVERNE is the obligated manufacturer is first made available in Germany:

- register the responsible legal entity in the Packaging Register LUCID
- ensure the legal name/address match the actual business entity
- enter the relevant brand name(s), including EVERNE where applicable
- keep the LUCID registration number in the compliance file

A company already registered in LUCID does not normally need a new registration merely because the law changed in August 2026, but its stored information may need updating.

## 3. System participation — BLOCKER

For packaging subject to system participation:

- conclude a system-participation contract with an approved system operator
- classify packaging by material type
- estimate/record packaging mass
- submit the agreed quantities to the system operator
- submit the required matching data to LUCID

Current ZSVR guidance states that packaging used for e-commerce shipping is system-participation packaging. Do not ship before the applicable participation obligation is fulfilled.

## 4. Quantity records — BLOCKER

For each packaging component record:

- material category
- unit weight in grams
- estimated annual unit count
- estimated annual kilograms by material
- who supplies/fills it
- which legal entity is the responsible manufacturer
- system operator used, where applicable

Keep supplier specs / invoices / weighing records that support the figures.

## 5. 2026 simplified reporting threshold

Current ZSVR guidance indicates a simplified reporting rule for manufacturers that placed less than 10 tonnes of system-participation packaging on the German market in the previous calendar year: instead of mirroring every system report immediately, a consolidated annual closing quantity declaration may be sufficient by 1 June of the following year.

Do not assume EVERNE qualifies automatically. Confirm the applicable reporting procedure in LUCID for the actual business volume and system contract.

## 6. PPWR technical documentation / conformity — BLOCKER WHERE EVERNE IS PRODUCER

The 2026 regime also requires checking who is the packaging `producer` responsible for technical documentation and the declaration of conformity under the PPWR.

For EVERNE-branded packaging obtain and retain, as applicable:

- packaging specification
- material composition
- supplier identification
- recyclability / material information
- technical documentation needed for the PPWR role
- declaration-of-conformity responsibility

Do not assume that paying a dual-system fee alone satisfies all PPWR producer obligations.

## 7. Care Set packaging

The Care Set creates an extra packaging layer beyond the four component products.

Before launch document:

- exact Care Set outer packaging
- inserts/dividers
- labels/stickers
- tissue/fill
- shipping carton/bag
- each material and unit weight
- whether component supplier packaging is retained or removed

The bundle may only launch after both the product compliance gate and packaging gate pass.

## 8. Dropship / third-party fulfilment caution

Do not assume a supplier or 3PL automatically fulfils EVERNE's obligations.

For the final fulfilment model obtain in writing:

- who physically fills the sales/shipping packaging
- whose brand is on the packaging
- which entity is legally responsible for registration/system participation
- applicable LUCID registration number
- system-participation evidence where another entity claims responsibility
- packaging-material/weight data needed for any EVERNE obligations

If the chain is unclear, keep commerce disabled.
