## Mandatory per-product commercial release gates

1. Supplier SKU maps correctly to **every offered** Shopify variant and current supplier source listing.
2. Supplier confirms real availability for Germany, full landed cost (product + shipping + taxes/fees where applicable), estimated shipping time and tracking.
3. At least one actual-sample inspection or equivalent reliable physical-product quality evidence. Ensure that customer-facing imagery is accurate; generated fashion images are *not* verified garment photographs.
4. Actual garment composition, fit/size chart, and German/EU consumer disclosures are verified, including return process and cost exposure.
5. Contribution after landed supplier cost, payment charges, shipping, and expected returns is positive and meets a separately approved risk buffer. Draft price alone is not margin evidence.
6. Test **automatic supplier payment** end-to-end without real customer orders or unsanctioned purchases. Order synchronization is not payment automation. If payment needs a manual action, this gate fails.
7. Confirm no duplicate storefront, no zero-inventory sale, correct variant colour/size controls, and explicit owner signoff for sale.

**Release condition:** All 15 pass all seven gates; only then separately authorize publishing product offers and enabling checkout.

## Operational blocker observed

EPROLO's published Shopify instructions describe **automatic order synchronization** but **payment completed separately on EPROLO**. This does not satisfy EVERNE's requirement of fully automatic supplier payment. Independent confirmation of a supported and tested automatic charging mechanism (or alternative provider) is required **before** any product can pass gate 6.

Evidence: https://eprolo.com/shopify-dropshipping/ and https://eprolo.com/de/project/order-fulfillment/ (reviewed 5 November 2026).
