## Recruitment

Invite **5–8 people** who might independently shop for women's clothing online, preferably outside the founder's close circle. Include at least three mobile participants; for the founder's device preference, prioritize one real iPhone Safari session. Do not offer incentives or incur costs without permission. Record whether the participant has ever heard of EVERNE; do not count friends' compliments as independent purchasing evidence.

Duration: ~15–20 minutes each. Give the link, explain that the preview sells nothing, and ask the participant to **think aloud**. Observe rather than lead. Do not tell them where to click. Written informed agreement is needed before taking video, voice, screenshots or personal contact details; by default collect anonymous, short written observations only.

## Moderator script (English site, German or English participant)

Say: *“This is an early version of an online clothing brand. Please use it as you normally would. I am evaluating the website, not you. You cannot buy anything here.”*

| Order | Task | Observer records |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Show only the first screen for ~5 seconds. Ask: “What is this website selling? Who do you think it is for?” | Comprehension and first words; never prime with “luxury” |
| 2 | “Find the black dress ideas.” | Category discovery, clicks, confused navigation, elapsed time |
| 3 | “Find the planned price of a top and tell me if it is a final selling price.” | Price comprehension and preview-status accuracy |
| 4 | “Find out what material, size and delivery time you would expect.” | Whether unknowns are correctly understood; misleading impressions |
| 5 | “Find who is behind the shop and what it checks before a garment is sold.” | Discoverability of Our World, Our Standard and legal notice |
| 6 | “Could you place a real order now? If not, why?” | User correctly identifies no checkout and unresolved supplier details |
| 7 | “What would you need to know or see before spending your own €49 here?” | Unprompted objections: actual photo, size, delivery, returns, independent proof |

## Scoring and release gate

- This is formative qualitative research, not a representative conversion-rate survey. Quote objections accurately and separately from moderator inference.
- Mark each navigational/comprehension task **pass / struggle / fail**, and tag severity: P0 misleading safety/commerce claim, P1 broken or undiscoverable essential task, P2 cosmetic friction.
- Repair all P0/P1 issues, then rerun the affected task with a *new* independent participant. Do not edit a respondent's recorded result retrospectively.
- Only after real products and checkout exist: run a **separate** purchase-journey acceptance with test payment and authorized staff. Do not substitute the prelaunch protocol for a paid checkout test.
- No production promotion until supplier/legal gates, independent usability evidence, Safari/mobile checks and founder written approval are all present.
