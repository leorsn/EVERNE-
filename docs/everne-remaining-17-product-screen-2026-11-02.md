## Cash screen: why plausible retail price is not verified profit

Assume an illustrative German Kleinunternehmer customer sale with full retail cash proceeds and **no output VAT deduction**. Apply a deliberately hypothetical, potentially nonrecoverable cost uplift to publicly listed supplier net goods, courier, 3-PLN-equivalent handling proxy (€0.68 net) and packaging (€0.70 net); add payment processing (2.5% of retail + €0.25) and a flat €2.50 return reserve:

- **Baseline:** 19% uplift on those net costs; **€7.50** net merchant-paid Poland→Germany label.
- **Stress:** 23% uplift on those net costs; **€10.00** net merchant-paid label.
- Contribution = retail − uplift × (public supplier net + courier + handling + packaging) − payment fee − return reserve. **This is not margin after advertising, overhead, defect returns, labor or tax advice.** Neither uplift nor courier amount is a quote or guaranteed tax treatment.

| Retail target | Maximum public net merchandise cost to retain **30% contribution under stress**, before overhead |
| ---: | ---: |
| €34 | €5.04 |
| €39 | €7.79 |
| €44 | €10.53 |
| €49 | €13.27 |
| €54 | €16.02 |
| €59 | €18.76 |

These ceilings are **rejection filters only**, not a promise of 30% real profit. The two sample-research leads clear the hypothetical stress-contribution threshold at their listed targets, but margin after all real costs is unknown. A larger basket, customer shipping charge or lower confirmed cross-border label rate could change the economics; do not invent these conditions. Actual Polish/EU supplier invoice tax treatment must be checked with tax advice for the seller's status.

## Merchant-specific evidence and release gates

1. Confirm the authentic merchant-specific FactoryPrice size/colour quote, number of orderable units, restock frequency, dropship permission and actual Shopify/BaseLinker integration costs.
2. Get a **real** own-label parcel quote and tracking/label requirements for one Poland→Germany consumer order. The wholesaler's advertised standard Germany shipping tariff is not this quote.
3. Authorize *samples separately* before spending. Photograph/touch-test material, seams, washed dimensions, lengths, opacity, styling and accurate grouped size conversion. Wool/recycled content must match garment labels before claims.
4. Obtain photo/description commercial rights or make original accurate images, plus manufacturer/textile/product-safety traceability and care labels.
5. Confirm merchant-funded German consumer cancellation/defect handling independently of FactoryPrice's short B2B-only returns (supplier says 7 working days on product pages); quantify unsellable refunds, return postage, integration overhead and customer support.
6. Connect Shopify, check existing handles and locations, then perform a zero-stock draft/test-order flow only after explicit approval. **No product import or publication is authorized by this research.**
