## Evidence required for every individual product

For each actual physical product to be sold, retain internally:

1. final supplier identity and invoice/source record
2. actual manufacturer name / registered trade name or trademark
3. manufacturer postal address
4. manufacturer electronic address
5. if manufacturer is outside the EU: EU responsible person's name, postal address and electronic address
6. product identification sufficient to tie the listing to the physical unit (model/type/batch/article number/SKU as applicable)
7. required warnings, safety instructions and user instructions for Germany
8. photographs of actual sample, packaging and markings
9. QC/sample acceptance record
10. mapping between actual sample and storefront photography/claims
11. record of any recall/safety notices known at launch

For online distance sales, required manufacturer/responsible-person/product-identification/warning information must be clearly and visibly available in the offer itself before purchase.

## Product-page publication requirements

Before each product becomes purchasable, its storefront data must include, clearly and visibly where applicable:

- product name and identifier/model
- manufacturer name/trade name/trademark
- manufacturer postal and electronic address
- EU responsible person details if manufacturer is outside the Union
- warnings/safety information required for Germany
- truthful key characteristics of the exact shipped product

Do not hide mandatory safety/economic-operator information solely inside a downloadable PDF or after checkout.

## Claims control

The following claim categories require direct evidence from the actual product/specification and must not be inferred from supplier photos:

- materials (e.g. beech wood, goat wool, copper/stainless steel)
- dimensions and weight
- wattage / voltage / charging method / battery chemistry
- suitability for cashmere, wool or other delicate fabrics
- reusable / disposable claims
- country of origin / manufacture
- certifications, FSC, CE or environmental claims
- compatibility with surfaces/materials
- package contents

If a claim cannot be tied to the final sample/source documentation, remove or soften it before launch.
