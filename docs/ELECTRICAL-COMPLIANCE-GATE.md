# EVERNE — Electrical / Electronic Product Compliance Gate

Status: **HARD BLOCKER FOR POWERED PRODUCTS**

Date: 29 September 2026

Applies at minimum to the current powered assortment:

- `EV-WH-01 / EV-WH-02 / EV-WH-03 / EV-WH-04` — Fabric Reviver Pro
- `EV-GB-01` — Steam Brush
- `EV-ECB-01 / EV-ECB-02 / EV-ECB-03` — Electric Cleaning Brush
- `EV-FR-01` — Fabric Reviver: exact physical mechanism/model must first be confirmed; if electrical/electronic, this gate applies in full

## Rule

Do not activate any powered SKU merely because a supplier listing shows a CE symbol or because Shopify inventory exists. Compliance must be tied to the **exact physical model actually sold**.

## 1. Exact product identity — BLOCKER

For each powered model obtain and retain:

- manufacturer
- exact model/type number
- product photographs including rating label
- rated voltage/frequency/power
- charging input/output where applicable
- battery type/capacity where applicable
- supplied plug/adapter details
- serial/batch/lot identification method
- actual package contents
- German-language instructions and safety warnings

## 2. EU conformity dossier — BLOCKER

Determine which harmonisation legislation applies to the exact model. Obtain and inspect the corresponding EU Declaration of Conformity and technical/compliance evidence. Depending on the product, this may include, among other things:

- Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU where its voltage scope is met
- EMC Directive 2014/30/EU
- RoHS Directive 2011/65/EU
- Radio Equipment Directive only if the device actually contains radio functionality
- battery-specific rules where a battery is included
- any other product-specific requirements applicable to the exact design

The declaration must identify the actual product/model and the responsible manufacturer. A generic certificate, unrelated model, marketplace screenshot or standalone CE logo is insufficient.

## 3. CE marking — BLOCKER WHERE REQUIRED

Verify on the actual sample and packaging/documentation:

- CE marking is present where applicable
- marking is legible and correctly placed
- manufacturer/model identification matches the declaration and sample
- required importer/responsible-person information is present as applicable

CE marking is a manufacturer's conformity declaration; it is not an EU authority safety approval. Do not advertise it as an independent quality award.

## 4. ElektroG / WEEE — BLOCKER

Before a powered device is first placed on the German market, determine whether EVERNE is a `Hersteller` under the ElektroG.

EVERNE may itself become the registration-responsible producer, for example if it:

- sells electrical/electronic equipment under its own name/brand in circumstances covered by ElektroG, or
- imports such equipment and first offers it on the German market.

If EVERNE is the obligated producer:

- obtain the necessary WEEE registration at stiftung ear **before** placing the device on the German market
- register the correct brand and device category
- retain the WEEE registration number and fulfil ongoing reporting/other obligations

If the upstream German/EU supplier is the registered producer instead, verify the exact registration/brand/model chain and retain evidence. Do not assume supplier responsibility without documentation.

## 5. Batteries — CONDITIONAL BLOCKER

For rechargeable/battery-powered models:

- identify chemistry and whether battery is built-in/removable
- identify the party responsible under current German/EU battery law
- verify required registration/EPR obligations before sale
- verify battery marking, safety, transport and end-user information as applicable

Do not classify a product as battery-free until the physical sample/specification proves it.

## 6. Product instructions and warnings — BLOCKER

For Germany, ensure adequate German-language instructions/safety information for foreseeable risks. Depending on the device, review at minimum:

### Steam Brush
- burn/scald and hot-surface warnings
- electrical/water-contact warnings
- permitted filling/use orientation
- fabric suitability cautions
- cooldown/storage instructions

### Fabric Reviver Pro
- moving/cutting/shaving head warnings
- use on flat/stable fabric
- loose-thread/seam/embellishment cautions
- charging/electrical safety
- cleaning and maintenance instructions

### Electric Cleaning Brush
- water-ingress/IP limits if any
- charging/electrical safety
- appropriate/inappropriate surfaces
- interchangeable-head use
- cleaning/maintenance and battery instructions

## 7. Sample acceptance — BLOCKER

For each model, photograph and record:

- front/back/sides
- rating label
- CE and disposal markings
- manufacturer/importer/responsible-person markings
- plug/charger
- packaging
- manual
- serial/model information

Compare all of these against the storefront listing and compliance documents.

## 8. Storefront rule

Until the full gate passes, powered products must remain unavailable even if:

- Shopify status is ACTIVE
- inventory is positive
- supplier stock is available
- the website has product photography

The commerce gate must not be used as a substitute for per-SKU compliance approval.
