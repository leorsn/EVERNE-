## Acceptance test for every sample

A sample passes only if all are true:
- physical product matches or can credibly replace current storefront imagery
- finish feels consistent with EVERNE positioning
- intended garment-care use is defensible
- no unacceptable supplier branding remains on the sellable unit
- manufacturer / responsible-party information is retained
- true landed cost stays within the current 40% gross-margin sourcing ceiling unless price is intentionally revised
- replenishment route is credible enough for launch

## Shopify rule after sample approval

Do not recreate products. Preserve product IDs, handles, URLs and pricing. Only after a sample passes:
- stop relying on the affected CJ inventory route
- assign actual received stock to the correct fulfilment location
- keep oversell protection enabled
- verify Germany shipping from that location
- run one real end-to-end test order before enabling purchase gates
