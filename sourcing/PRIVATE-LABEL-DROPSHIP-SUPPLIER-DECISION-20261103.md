# EVERNE — Sewn-label + automatic-payment supplier decision

**Date:** 3 November 2026. **Status:** supplier market verification only / **NO-GO** for all 20 Shopify draft concepts. **New spend authorised:** €0. **Live automation authorised:** no.

## Non-negotiable acceptance requirements

1. A physical EVERNE **sewn brand label** on every garment (including trousers, denim, skirts and dresses), not just a hangtag, printed neck graphic or size relabel.
2. True one-item customer-order dropshipping with no required upfront inventory or expensive label batch.
3. Shopify customer orders → exact supplier size/color product → **automatic supplier payment** → fulfillment/tracking, with manageable exception and spend limits.
4. Deliver to German customers with predictable merchant-paid customs/tax, a working 14-day consumer withdrawal path, and viable per-item economics.
5. No additional subscription or paid order before an express owner-approved spend, and no live product activation without physical samples + legal/photography checks.

## Most significant new finding

**CJ's tiny $0.20 "Relabeling Fee" on public clothing listings is not an EVERNE branding price.** Its May–July 2026 Label Removal & Replacement procedure is expressly a **size-label mismatch correction**, with a new target size chosen by the merchant. It should never be counted as bespoke sewn brand-label service. That closes an earlier ambiguity in the public source-product pages.

**Apliiq is the clearest no-minimum sewn-label + automatic-card-billing option actually documented in public vendor terms**, but its standard private label placement is in a neck. It does not solve our denim/trouser/skirts label problem as advertised, and its US-centric international freight/import billing is risky for a German premium customer journey.

**BuckyDrop remains the broadest prospective all-fashion candidate**, and **CJ the lowest-friction installed-app pilot**. Neither has verified merchant-specific sewn-label quotes, Germany door-delivered all-in pricing or a 14-day returns route. Do not choose one as approved supplier yet.

### EU-provider reality check (new evidence)

- **AJDN:** actual sewn application €2 excl. VAT per garment, but their own woven-label manufacture starts at **250 units**. Customer-provided labels would still have to exist and be available in their facility. Crucially, Shopify *order import* is not proof of *automatic supplier payment*.
- **Caspar:** the seemingly attractive €0.80 garment-label operation hides an advance **50-label satin** or **500-label woven** minimum; labels are stored at Caspar and applied to future per-unit orders. €4 + VAT per EU single-item shipment is a verified starting figure, but the exact EVERNE cut/fit and supplier billing are open.
- **Fabrixa:** genuinely single-unit EU production with an order-deducted prepaid wallet; the catalogue is print-led, not the existing unprinted fashion collection, and the company does **not** accept retailer/customer preference returns. This is not our intended apparel supplier.
- **GoCustom:** old system currently does not accept new registrations. Its former sew-in service is therefore not a launch path.

**Finding:** After screening eleven named vendors, none is publicly evidenced as meeting **every** EVERNE launch requirement simultaneously across **all twenty exact fashion concepts**. Supplier marketing claims such as "no MOQ" often describe garment fulfillment or packaging; they do **not** waive the manufacturing minimum for a physically sewn custom woven label. No false product-to-supplier match should be imported into Shopify.

### Single decisive quote request (not sent; no contact permission)

Request the following **for all categories**, beginning with one **black straight-leg denim**, one **tailored trouser**, and one **black ribbed knit**:

1. A real catalog URL and stable SKU for the **exact garment, size and color**, with size chart, material composition, photos/usage rights and currently available stock.
2. Manufacturer/retailer brand-label removal and **physical sewn EVERNE fabric brand label** at a non-neck position for pants and skirts. State **minimum garment order, minimum label production, prepaid material stock, unit sewing fee, artwork/setup costs**, and whether labels remain stored for future orders.
3. Germany **all-in per-order cost** at quantity 1: garment, source shipping, label manufacture amortisation, sewing, service fee, international delivery, DDP/import/VAT handling and potential surcharges. Quote realistic processing and delivery windows.
4. Shopify integration proof for **order → supplier payment without clicking Pay/Confirm → fulfillment/tracking**, with prepaid-wallet/card requirement, low-balance behavior, spend cap and exception processing.
5. A named EU **returns destination**, who processes the consumer's 14-day withdrawal, exact handling/postage/refund fees, unsold-returned-stock treatment, product-defect procedure, and evidence of required textile/fiber/manufacturer compliance.

**Approval path:** verify written offer → evaluate at least one real sample/label and the actual delivery → test a no-live-commerce Shopify order in a restricted flow → owner signs off an exact SKU → only then activate. This research does **not** authorize a quotation email, app install, payment-method connection, wallet funding, sample purchase, or product publication.
