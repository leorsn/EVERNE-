# EVERNE — Sewn-label + automatic-payment supplier decision

**Date:** 3 November 2026. **Status:** supplier market verification only / **NO-GO** for all 20 Shopify draft concepts. **New spend authorised:** €0. **Live automation authorised:** no.

## Non-negotiable acceptance requirements

1. A physical EVERNE **sewn brand label** on every garment (including trousers, denim, skirts and dresses), not just a hangtag, printed neck graphic or size relabel.
2. True one-item customer-order dropshipping with no required upfront inventory or expensive label batch.
3. Shopify customer orders → exact supplier size/color product → **automatic supplier payment** → fulfillment/tracking, with manageable exception and spend limits.
4. Deliver to German customers with predictable merchant-paid customs/tax, a working 14-day consumer withdrawal path, and viable per-item economics.
5. No additional subscription or paid order before an express owner-approved spend, and no live product activation without physical samples + legal/photography checks.

## Most significant new finding

**CJ's tiny $0.20 "Relabeling Fee" on public clothing listings is not an EVERNE branding price.** Its May–July 2026 Label Removal & Replacement procedure is expressly a **size-label mismatch correction**, with a new target size chosen by the merchant. It should never be counted as bespoke sewn brand-label service. That closes an earlier ambiguity in the public source-product pages.

**Apliiq is the clearest no-minimum sewn-label + automatic-card-billing option actually documented in public vendor terms**, but its standard private label placement is in a neck. It does not solve our denim/trouser/skirts label problem as advertised, and its US-centric international freight/import billing is risky for a German premium customer journey.

**BuckyDrop remains the broadest prospective all-fashion candidate**, and **CJ the lowest-friction installed-app pilot**. Neither has verified merchant-specific sewn-label quotes, Germany door-delivered all-in pricing or a 14-day returns route. Do not choose one as approved supplier yet.
