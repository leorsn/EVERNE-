# EVERNE — Legal Launch Checklist

Status: PRE-LAUNCH — orders disabled

## Blockers before checkout activation

- [ ] Replace the imprint placeholder with the final serviceable business address.
- [ ] Replace the contact-email placeholder with the final EVERNE business email.
- [ ] Confirm final operator/legal form and update the imprint if this changes.
- [ ] Add VAT ID / business ID / register information if and when legally applicable.
- [ ] Confirm final supplier and fulfilment model.
- [ ] Publish final delivery countries, delivery times and shipping costs.
- [ ] Publish final return address and return-cost allocation.
- [ ] Verify product safety information and responsible economic operator data for every product.
- [ ] Verify packaging-law responsibilities for the final fulfilment model.

## Privacy / cookies

- [x] No marketing, remarketing or analytics tags intentionally enabled in the current pre-launch storefront.
- [x] Legal page states current cookie/consent status.
- [ ] Before adding Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Analytics or equivalent: inventory all technologies and add a consent platform that blocks non-essential tags until consent.
- [ ] Update privacy notice with actual hosting, Shopify, payment, analytics, marketing and international-transfer details.
- [ ] Verify data-processing agreements and provider settings.

## Shopify checkout verification

Do not set `PURCHASES_ENABLED` to `true` until all items below are verified in the real checkout.

- [ ] Delivery restrictions are clearly shown no later than the start of the ordering process.
- [ ] Accepted payment methods are clearly shown no later than the start of the ordering process.
- [ ] Immediately before order submission, the legally required product, price and contract information is clearly and prominently displayed.
- [ ] The final order button unambiguously indicates that the order creates a payment obligation.
- [ ] Total price and all additional shipping/other costs are transparent before order submission.
- [ ] Terms, privacy notice, withdrawal information and shipping information remain readily accessible.
- [ ] Order confirmation and required contractual information are sent on a durable medium.
- [ ] Electronic withdrawal function is live, prominently accessible during the withdrawal period, and sends the required receipt confirmation.
- [ ] Test order completed successfully on mobile and desktop.

## Final go-live gate

EVERNE may only enable purchases after all unchecked legal, fulfilment and checkout blockers above have been resolved and the live Shopify checkout has been tested end-to-end.
